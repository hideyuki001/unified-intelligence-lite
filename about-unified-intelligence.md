# About Unified Intelligence Lite v3.0

## 🧠 What is this framework?

Unified Intelligence Lite is not just a set of tools or prompts.  
It is a **framework for structuring thought itself** — a way to formalize and modularize cognitive styles.

Instead of asking *"what is the correct answer?"*, it asks:  
**"how did the reasoning process unfold?"**

---

## 🎯 Why "Cognitive Design"?

Traditional prompts focus on outputs:
- Translate this sentence
- Summarize this article
- Score this essay

Unified Intelligence modules focus on **thinking methods**:
- `translation-qa.md`: Translation is judged by semantic alignment, structural fidelity, and fluency.  
- `recursive-qa.md`: Evaluation must include self-critique and revision.  
- `is-lm-summary.md`: Economic reasoning is modeled as shifts within IS/LM equilibrium.  
- `poetic-core.md`: Language is compressed into symbolic/metaphorical cores.  

This shifts the focus from *"what was said"* to *"how the model thought."*

---

## 🔍 Core Principles

1. **Reproducible Thought Structures**  
   Every `.md` file encodes a reusable reasoning template.  
   Anyone — human or LLM — can load and apply the same thought style.

2. **Process over Output**  
   Value is placed on **the reasoning chain**, not just the final result.

3. **Recursive Self-Improvement**  
   Modules allow outputs to be re-evaluated, re-scored, and refined.

4. **Lightweight & Human-Readable**  
   Markdown files = minimal, portable, and transparent.  
   They can be studied as documents *and* executed as prompts.

---

## 🌐 Why It Matters

- Builds a **common language of reasoning** between humans and LLMs.  
- Turns prompts from one-off instructions into **persistent cognitive frameworks**.  
- Creates knowledge assets that can be reused, refined, and extended.  

This is not a "tool" — it is a **package of thought-design**.

---

## 📜 Position of this Release

This is the **Lite version**:  
- Focused on translation, recursion, symbolic reasoning, and a single macroeconomic model.  
- Other advanced modules (VAR, risk parity, multimodal analysis, etc.) are excluded.  

The purpose is to demonstrate the **value of structured cognitive templates** without exposing the entire system.

**Unified Intelligence is the software-ization of thinking styles — cognitive engineering for the LLM era.**
